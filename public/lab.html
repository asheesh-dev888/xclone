5 · Findings (honest version)

  • 3x = ex·ln3. Every “base-3” network is exactly a base-e network whose logits are scaled by ln 3 ≈ 1.0986 (temperature ≈ 0.910). Nothing fundamental changes.
  • Any small speed-up you see in the trainer comes from that ln 3 scaling: slightly sharper probabilities and an effectively larger step size. Tick match temperature and the base-e net gets its LR scaled to match; the gap mostly disappears (and on a different seed the order can flip).
  • With Adam, the scale-invariance of updates makes the two nearly identical; with SGD the effect is larger but still just a learning-rate/temperature knob.
  • Hardware: 3x is computed as exp(x·ln3), one extra multiply. It costs the same as ex; no efficiency is gained.
  • e is “natural” because d/dx eˣ = eˣ; with base 3 every derivative picks up a ln 3 factor. Growth with base 3 is just growth at rate r·ln3.
